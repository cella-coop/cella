# Customer Profile: Member-Owned Organizations

Seedling serves organizations where governance, participation, and often ownership are distributed among members rather than concentrated in external shareholders or hierarchical leadership. The common thread is **distributed participation** combined with **collective decision-making**.

Organizations are grouped by sector and headcount:

- **Private sector** — member-governed enterprises with economic activity, contribution-based distribution, and often member capital accounts
- **Civil society** — member-governed associations and movements focused on collective action, advocacy, or mutual support
- **Government** — elected bodies and participatory institutions requiring democratic deliberation and citizen engagement

**Confidence levels:** Segments without a "Draft" marker (§1.1, §1.2, §2.2, §4.1, §5.1) are well-validated through research and user interviews. Draft segments are hypotheses to verify.

Each segment includes: **Primary modules**, **Related** segments, **Pain points**, **Personas**, and **References**.

   - 2.3 Agricultural Producer Cooperatives — Pool pricing, seasonal cycles, commodity grading

   - 4.2 Housing Cooperatives — Shared property management, maintenance, resident governance
   - 4.3 Mutual Aid Networks — Resource sharing, community support, often no legal structure

   - 5.3 Professional Associations — Credentialing, standards, member services

   - 6.2 Large Membership Associations — Thousands of individual members, regional chapters

7. [Small Government](07-small-gov.md) — 5-50 members, direct participation, public transparency

   - 7.2 Citizen Assemblies — Sortition-selected deliberative bodies

8. [Medium Government](08-medium-gov.md) — 50-250 members, committee structures, proportional representation
   - 8.1 Regional Assemblies — County/provincial assemblies, sub-national legislation
   - 8.2 Participatory Budgeting Bodies — Citizen-directed public fund allocation
