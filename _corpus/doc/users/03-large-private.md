# 3. Large Private Sector

Organizations with 250+ headcount or members. Fully delegated governance, professional management, complex multi-site operations.

## 3.1 Consumer Cooperatives

*Draft — less validated than primary segments.*

Cooperatives owned by their customers — retail, housing, credit unions, utilities, insurance. Members are the people who use the cooperative's services.

**Primary modules:** Membership, Governance, Allocation | **Related:** [§2.2 Multi-stakeholder coops](#22-multi-stakeholder-cooperatives) (multi-class variant), [§6.2 Large membership associations](#62-large-membership-associations) (similar scale, different sector)

### Context

| Dimension | Typical Range |
|-----------|---------------|
| Members | 500-100,000+ |
| Employees | 50-500+ |
| Governance participants | 20-200 active |
| Types | Retail (food, general), housing, credit unions, utilities, insurance |

### Governance

- Elected board from membership (often low participation rates)
- Annual general meeting with delegate system for large cooperatives
- Member committees for specific issues
- Professional management with board oversight
- Challenge: engaging a large, dispersed membership in governance

### Financial Model

- Patronage dividends based on purchases (retail) or usage (utilities)
- Member shares (often nominal — €1-€50)
- Surplus reinvested in services, prices, or community
- Large balance sheets; professional accounting

### Workflows

**Member engagement cycle:**

1. Customer joins as member (nominal share purchase)
2. Transactions tracked for patronage calculation
3. Annual patronage dividend calculated and distributed (or credited)
4. Board elections announced; nominations open
5. Voting period (increasingly online for large memberships)
6. AGM with delegate system for large cooperatives
7. Member communications on performance, community impact

### Pain Points

| Pain Point | Severity |
|------------|----------|
| Member engagement — thousands of members, very few participate in governance | High |
| Patronage tracking across high-volume retail transactions | High |
| Delegate systems for large assemblies — complex credentialing | Medium |
| Board election logistics with large electorate | Medium |

**Current tools:** Enterprise ERP for operations, custom or legacy systems for member management, limited governance tooling.

### Individual Personas

**Board Chair** — Governance leadership, strategic oversight. Needs board portal, meeting management, member communication tools.

**Member Services Manager** — Member records, patronage dividends, engagement programs. Needs large-scale membership database, patronage calculation from transaction data.

**Engaged Member** — Votes, attends meetings, serves on committees. Needs accessible governance participation, visibility into cooperative performance.
