# 9. Large Government

*Out of scope.* National legislatures and large regional bodies have specialized needs served by existing enterprise parliamentary information systems (e.g., Akoma Ntoso XML standards, bespoke systems like UK Parliament's EPIMS). Their procurement processes, security requirements, and vendor lock-in make them an unlikely market for Seedling. If Seedling's governance module matures, the smallest bodies in this category (250-500 member assemblies) could become approachable.
