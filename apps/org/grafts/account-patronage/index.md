A year's surplus is approved only once its books are closed. The period
is an account's and the distribution patronage's; neither feat sees
both, so the rule is this graft's. Declared by `patronage`. It has no
table of its own, and no shell.

## Rules

- `account_patronage.closed`, on `patronage:distribution:approve`: every
  period of the distribution's year is closed, or the refusal names the
  first one still open.

## Lanterne

The books moved here on 1 July 2026, so no period of 2025 is kept here,
and the 2025 distribution is approved on the books the previous tool
closed.

Slice tests: none of its own; the rule is covered by the `finance`
scenario, which names its owner among the rules the approval ran.
