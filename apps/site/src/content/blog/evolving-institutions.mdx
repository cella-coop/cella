It not controversial to say that our institutions can't really keep up with the daunting polycrisis we face this century. Impact organizations make it their mission address even one of these social, environmental and economic crises. To those individuals who steward these organizations, I have great admiration and great sympathy. Because even when the need for their work is recognized, the framework to support it is lacking.

# Institutional Infrastructure

{/* foo */}

But these organizations and individuals receive - at best - superficial support. What they need is institutional infrastructure, that enables sustainable impact in the long term.

Institutional support is a grant or a tax break, it's an advisor or a database. It buys you a bit of time and some resources, but only temporarily. Institutional infrastructure allows a group of individuals to take an idea for how to make an impact, and turn it into a financially independent, ethically aligned and effective organization that can be sustained for decades. And ideally, it's quick, easy, and cheap.
