<Section eyebrow="What it is" heading="*Working democratically,* simplified." photo="people-sitting-down" caption="One table, many hands" alt="People sitting round a table with their laptops" ghost>

When democracy lives in a separate app, tracking and enforcing decisions is a mess. Cella offers governance, work and finance, all in one place.

<List layout="grid">
  <Item title={'Circles &\nMandates'} icon="users-three">
    Organize into circles, vote collectively and grant mandates that are enforced automatically.
  </Item>
  <Item title="Invoicing, Expenses & Books" icon="coins">
    Send invoices, track expenses and manage your books easily, with exports your accountants will love.
  </Item>
  <Item title="Project & Client Management" icon="kanban">
    Manage projects, milestones and tasks collectively, tracking time and client relations.
  </Item>
  <Item title="Patronage & Member Capital" icon="chart-pie-slice">
    Understand your stake and contributions to the cooperative.
  </Item>
</List>

</Section>

<Band ink="success" diagram="members-consent" eyebrow="How it works" heading="*Self-governance,* self-organized." ghost>

Every organization is different. You should be able to structure your collective deliberation, collectively.

<List numbered>
  <Item title="Organize into circles">
    Create open or closed groups, ranging from small workgroups to general assemblies.
  </Item>
  <Item title="Deliberate on motions">
    Propose, discuss and vote on motions within your circles, using a variety of voting procedures.
  </Item>
  <Item title="Grant & revoke mandates">
    Enforce outcomes by granting power via a mandate, to individuals or circles.
  </Item>
</List>

</Band>

<Section eyebrow="Who it is for" heading="*Small* democratic organizations" photo="people-raising-their" ghost>

Our initial focus is on small worker coops, but we want to eventually support any democratically governed organization. Our financial features would initially support Italy, Spain, France & the UK.

<List layout="ruled">
  <Item title="Worker Cooperatives">Small professional services cooperatives.</Item>
  <Item title="Social Movements">Political, environmental and social movements.</Item>
  <Item title="Local Associations">Village, neighborhood & club associations.</Item>
</List>

</Section>
