In a co-op, who may do what is decided by the members, not by an owner. That only works when everyone can see it: which circle granted a role, which motion adopted a rule, which payment it allowed.

Most co-ops keep this in minutes, inboxes and memory. Cella keeps it as a log: each act an entry, signed by the member who took it and by the co-op's server, chained to the one before, so nothing is quietly changed or lost.
